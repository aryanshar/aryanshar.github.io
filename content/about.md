I'm a Senior AI/ML Engineer at **Jaguar Land Rover** in Bengaluru, where I've
spent nearly five years building production deep-learning systems for
autonomous driving — and the infrastructure that keeps them alive.

## What I work on

**3D perception.** I built an in-house monocular 3D object-detection network
that lifted 2D-to-3D localization accuracy from 55% to 91%, now deployed as the
core API behind our internal Ground Truth System. I've also worked on LiDAR
point-cloud panoptic segmentation, using Segment Anything masks and kNN spatial
clustering in a Ray-served auto-labelling pipeline.

**Semantic data curation.** I built Scene Search from scratch — a platform that
uses CLIP/VLM embeddings with Qdrant over a Voxel51 UI, cutting
target-scenario discovery time from around 50 hours to roughly 75 minutes.

**ML platform & MLOps.** Reusable Ray distributed-training framework on
on-demand EKS clusters with automatic teardown, enterprise infrastructure
templated with AWS CDK, Argo Workflows for orchestration, and a unified
OpenTelemetry + Grafana observability stack spanning on-prem DGX systems and
AWS.

## Background

B.Tech–M.Tech dual degree in Aerospace Engineering from **IIT Kanpur**, where I
co-authored a pre-print on lightweight multi-drone detection and 3D
localization via YOLO — the work that became my
[swarm-detection](https://github.com/aryanshar/swarm-detection) dataset and
pipeline.

Along the way: global winner of the JLR Hackathon (2023) for a camera-only
360° object-detection architecture, an Exceptional Creator Award (2024), and a
selection to present agentic MLOps work at JLR UK (2025).
