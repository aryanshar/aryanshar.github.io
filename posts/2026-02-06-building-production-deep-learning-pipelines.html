Building production-ready deep learning pipelines

February 6, 2026 · AI/ML

This post outlines practical steps for taking deep learning models from research to production: data versioning, training pipelines, model validation, monitoring, and serving strategies.

Key points

  • Reproducible experiments with deterministic seeds and artifact tracking.
  • Use of feature stores for consistent training/serving features.
  • Canary and shadow deployments for safe rollouts.
  • Continuous monitoring for data drift and model performance.
